GoldTrace records each change to your gold and files it under one of four labels.

Clean

Earned from the game: quests, loot, vendor sales and normally priced auctions.

Untracked

Held before you installed the addon, or gained while it was switched off.

Unknown origin

Received from a character that doesn't run GoldTrace, so its history can't be seen.

Flagged

Large one-sided gifts from strangers, or auction sales far above a sensible price.
